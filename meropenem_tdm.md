# Meropenem Therapeutic Drug Monitoring (TDM)

## Source: International consensus recommendations for therapeutic drug monitoring of beta-lactam antibiotics
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30307434/
**Year:** 2019

## Key Recommendations
- Target free drug concentrations above MIC for the majority of the dosing interval
- For meropenem, consider extended or continuous infusion in critically ill patients
- Trough levels are not reliable for beta-lactams; consider 2-4 hour post-infusion levels
- TDM is recommended for patients with augmented renal clearance, obesity, renal impairment, or ECMO
- Therapeutic target: fT>MIC of 40-50% for most infections, 100% for meningitis

## Extended Infusion Considerations
- Extended infusion (3-hour) may improve pharmacodynamic target attainment
- Particularly useful for organisms with elevated MICs
- Requires clinical review and pharmacy coordination
